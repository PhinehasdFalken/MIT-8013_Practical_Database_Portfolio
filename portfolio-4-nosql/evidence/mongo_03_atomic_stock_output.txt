A4  Limitation (and why payments stay in PostgreSQL)
======================================================================
A sale touches the PRODUCT document (stock) AND creates an ORDER document. Those are TWO documents.
Atomicity across several documents needs a multi-document transaction, which in MongoDB requires a
replica set and costs performance - the BASE trade-off described in the course. Finesse Klass therefore
keeps orders/payments in the ACID relational database (Portfolio 3) and uses MongoDB for the catalogue.
